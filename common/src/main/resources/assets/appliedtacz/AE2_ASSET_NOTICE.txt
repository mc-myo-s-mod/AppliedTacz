Applied Energistics 2 Molecular Assembler exterior and glass

The outer frame, inset glass geometry and UV mapping in models/block/tacz_assembler.json
are adapted from Applied Energistics 2's molecular_assembler.json (19.2.16).
The perimeter beams are partitioned to retain AppliedTaCZ's existing inner
frame without overlap. The inset center cube supplies the original glass.
The online-only prismatic light overlay directly uses AE2's registered
block/molecular_assembler_lights model and its animated texture at runtime.

Source: https://github.com/AppliedEnergistics/Applied-Energistics-2
Asset: src/main/resources/assets/ae2/models/block/molecular_assembler.json
