Applied Energistics 2 Molecular Assembler exterior and glass

The outer frame, inset glass geometry and UV mapping in models/block/tacz_assembler.json
are adapted from Applied Energistics 2's molecular_assembler.json (19.2.16).
The perimeter beams are partitioned to retain AppliedTaCZ's existing inner
frame without overlap. The inset center cube supplies the original glass.
The online-only prismatic light overlay directly uses AE2's registered
block/molecular_assembler_lights model and its animated texture at runtime.

Source: https://github.com/AppliedEnergistics/Applied-Energistics-2
Asset: src/main/resources/assets/ae2/models/block/molecular_assembler.json

Applied Energistics 2 Pattern Encoding Terminal editable assets

The loader-specific textures/part/pattern_encoding_terminal_{bright,medium,dark}.png
are editable copies of AE2's pattern encoding terminal screen layers.
Sources: appliedenergistics2-forge-15.4.10.jar (Minecraft 1.20.1) and
appliedenergistics2-19.2.17.jar (Minecraft 1.21.1).
Small local part/item JSON wrappers bind these screen layers while inheriting AE2's
geometry. Frames, status indicators, item display transforms and their common textures
are supplied directly by the installed AE2 version, not copied into this mod.
The screen PNG copies retain their original pixels and the above AE2 asset credits and
CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 license; they are not independently authored AppliedTaCZ artwork.
